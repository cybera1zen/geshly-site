Ground rules

What we aim to do, and won't do.

We aim to

  • Link every entry to its original public source
  • Show when a post was written and when we collected it
  • Respect each source's rules and remove content on request
  • Say clearly what is built and what isn't

We won't

  • Invent testimonials, customer logos or user counts
  • Make up problems and present them as real
  • Sell or publish people's private details
  • Ask for payment before there is a product to use

Questions

Straight answers.

Is geshly.site live?

No. Only this preview page exists. The searchable problem database is still being planned and built.

Where will the problems come from?

From public posts and reviews on the web, such as forums, app reviews and community threads, using sources whose terms allow it. The exact sources aren't final. User-submitted problems are also planned.

Will you store my personal information?

Not through this page. The email box is a preview and sends nothing. For the product, the plan is to keep public text only and not collect people's names or contact details.

Are the examples on this page real?

No. They are labelled as illustrative.

What does it cost?

Nothing is for sale yet. The plan is one payment in US dollars, planned at $97 and not final.
