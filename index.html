Ground rules

What this is, and isn't.

What you get

  • Live tab: real Ask HN posts fetched from Hacker News' official search API when you search. Nothing is stored by us.
  • Every live result links to the original post, with its date.
  • Sample tab: made-up examples, marked as samples, with no source because none exists.
  • Our plan is to add more sources whose terms allow it.

What it isn't (yet)

  • Not a saved database. We don't store or rank posts yet.
  • Not a measure of demand. Points and comments are Hacker News' numbers, not ours.
  • Not a source of invented testimonials, customer logos or user counts.
  • Nothing is for sale. No payment is taken.

Questions

Straight answers.

Where do live results come from?

Hacker News, through its official public search API (hn.algolia.com). Results are posts of type "Ask HN" matching your words. Content belongs to its authors; each result links to the original.

Are the sample entries real?

No. They are invented to show the idea and are labelled "Sample" on every card.

What about other sources like Reddit or app reviews?

Not included. We only use sources whose terms allow automated collection, and we'll say which ones when they're added.

Do you store my searches?

No. Searching runs in your browser and sends your query to Hacker News' search API. This site has no backend and no analytics.

What will it cost?

Nothing is for sale yet. A one-time price of around $97 was an early idea and is not final.

Want it when it grows up?

More sources, saved problems and "me too" votes are planned. Join the early access list.
